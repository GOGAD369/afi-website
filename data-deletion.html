Data Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

You can ask Afi Digital Solutions to delete the data we hold about you or your business. This applies to our products, including AFI Connect and AFI Gym, and to any data we received through Facebook Login or Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to gogadafiofficial@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +91 93849 26539.
  2. Tell us the name of your business, and the email address or phone number you used to sign up.
  3. We may ask you to confirm that the request is really from you, to keep your data safe.

What happens next

  • We delete your account and the data linked to it, including your connected WhatsApp account details, messages stored on our platform, contacts and settings.
  • We complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming your request, and we will tell you when it is done.
  • We may keep a small amount of information where the law requires it, for example payment and tax records.

Your WhatsApp account at Meta

Deleting your data with us does not delete your WhatsApp Business Account, your phone number or your Facebook account. Those belong to you and are managed in Meta's own settings. You can also remove our app's access at any time in your Meta Business settings.

If you are a customer of a business that uses our services

If a business messaged you through our platform, you can ask that business to delete your data, or contact us at the email above and we will help get your request to them.

More about how we handle data is in our Privacy Policy.
